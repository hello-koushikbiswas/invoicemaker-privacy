Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Invoice Studio ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by KB DevStudio, handles your information.

1. Data Storage & Offline Architecture

Invoice Studio is designed with an offline-first architecture. All client records, invoice items, invoice drafts, business details, currency settings, and logos entered into the app are stored exclusively on your device's local storage (via AsyncStorage / local file systems).

We do not operate backend servers that collect, store, transmit, or synchronize your personal or financial data. Your business and client information never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

2. Document Generation and Sharing

When you generate PDF invoices or receipts using the app's features (such as expo-print and expo-sharing), the PDF documents are created locally on your device. Any subsequent sharing via email, messaging applications, or cloud drives is executed entirely through your device's native sharing sheet under your direct control.

3. In-App Purchases & Billing Information

Invoice Studio offers an optional lifetime upgrade (Studio Pro). All financial transactions and in-app purchases are handled directly by the Google Play Billing system. We do not process, collect, or store any credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or billing addresses on our own systems. Google manages your payment information under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

The app may request access to:

  • Storage / Media: To allow you to pick custom company logos from your device photo library and save exported PDF invoices.
  • Network / Billing Access: Strictly to verify and process in-app purchase entitlements via Google Play services.

5. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

Invoice Studio does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or tracking analytics. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to data brokers or third parties.

6. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the application or clearing the application data through Android Settings permanently erases all saved invoices, business records, and preferences.
